Append the reader's note to today's deep dive.

1. Find today's brief directory: `data/briefs/<today>/`.
2. Append to `notes.md` in that directory, creating it if needed, with a timestamped
   heading and the paper title.
3. Write the note in the reader's own framing -- record what *they* concluded or
   asked, not a summary of what you said. If the note settles a question that was
   open in the deep dive, say which one.

Keep notes in Traditional Chinese with English technical terms, matching the deep
dive convention.
